# url2regex extension privacy policy

The url2regex Chrome extension converts the URLs or paths you paste into its
popup into a regular expression. Processing takes place locally in your browser.

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your input or generated
patterns. It does not use analytics, advertising, accounts, or tracking services.
It does not save your input or patterns to browser storage. The popup does not
retain its contents after it closes.

The extension does not request access to browsing history, tabs, website
contents, or other Chrome extension permissions. Its code and assets are bundled
with the extension; it does not load remote code.

This policy describes the extension itself. Chrome and the Chrome Web Store
operate under Google's own policies.

For privacy questions, open an issue at
https://github.com/mertefeisawesome/url2regex/issues. Avoid including private URLs
or other sensitive information in public issues.
